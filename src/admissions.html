Fall 2026 Admissions Open

The university is pleased to announce that admissions for Fall 2026 semester are now open for all undergraduate and graduate programs.

Students are encouraged to apply online through the official admission portal. Make sure to upload all required documents before the deadline.

Required Documents

  • Passport size photographs
